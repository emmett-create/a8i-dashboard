Privacy Policy

A8I — effective November 17, 2026

* * *

A8I is built and operated by Agency Eight. This policy explains what data A8I accesses, why, and what happens to it — specifically covering the Gmail access A8I uses to send outreach emails on a connected account's behalf and to read replies to those emails.

What we access, and why

When an Agency Eight team member or an A8I client connects a Gmail account, A8I requests two Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send — used only to send outreach emails (initial outreach and follow-ups) from the connected account to creators/brands on behalf of the account owner. A8I never sends anything other than the outreach messages the account owner has configured.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly — used only to find and read replies to those specific outreach emails, so A8I can classify each reply (for example: agreed, declined, asked about payment, needs human review) and surface it back to the account owner. A8I does not read, search, or otherwise access any other email in the account.

What we store

A8I does not copy or store the contents of any email. Reply data is read live from Gmail each time it's needed, held in memory only long enough to generate a classification, and then discarded — it is never written to a database, log file, or backup.

The only things A8I stores are: (1) the OAuth credentials needed to stay connected to the account the owner authorized, kept on Agency Eight's own server infrastructure and never shared with a third party except as described below, and (2) the small set of outreach metadata the account owner enters themselves (e.g. a creator's name and email address, to know who to send to).

Who else sees it

Reply content is passed, transiently and only at the moment a reply needs classifying, to Anthropic's Claude API as our AI sub-processor, solely to generate the classification label described above. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models under Agency Eight's commercial terms with Anthropic. No other third party receives email content.

Who can access this inside Agency Eight

Only Agency Eight's operations team members working directly on A8I have access to the connected accounts' credentials and the resulting classification output.

Revoking access

Any connected account owner can revoke A8I's access at any time from their Google Account's Security & third-party access settings. Revoking access immediately stops A8I from sending or reading mail on that account.

Changes to this policy

If what A8I accesses or how it's used changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.
